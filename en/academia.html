Efficacy and Safety of Guselkumab in Patients with Crohn's Disease and Ulcerative Colitis: A Prospective Real-World Study

Shafrir A, Choi D, Capucci LC, Hannett A, Tanouye J, Mathew AJ, Natt N, et al. · Clinical Gastroenterology and Hepatology, 2026

GI

Prospective real-world study of 234 adults (142 with Crohn's disease, 92 with ulcerative colitis) who started guselkumab at a large tertiary IBD center and were followed for up to a year. About 77% of Crohn's and 81% of ulcerative colitis patients were in clinical remission at week 12, remission was maintained at six months in roughly 85–88%, and adverse events were mostly mild — a safety profile similar to the pivotal trials.

Transition to Subcutaneous Vedolizumab Is Effective and Safe in Adults with IBD in Clinical Remission, Including Those on Dose-Escalated Intravenous Vedolizumab

Natt N, Shafrir A, Fine Z, et al. · Journal of the Canadian Association of Gastroenterology, 2026

GI

Prospective cohort of 86 patients in clinical remission who switched from intravenous to subcutaneous vedolizumab, including those on dose-escalated IV dosing. One-year treatment persistence was 82.6% with no significant difference between standard and dose-escalated groups, and 97.5% of patients in remission at baseline were still in remission at one year.
