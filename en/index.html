Gastroenterology · Jerusalem

Asher Shafrir, M.D.

Senior Gastroenterologist · Hadassah & Meuhedet

I am a senior physician in the Gastroenterology Department at Hadassah–Hebrew University Medical Center and at Meuhedet Health Services. I trained in internal medicine and gastroenterology at Hadassah, followed by an advanced fellowship in inflammatory bowel disease at the University of Chicago. My clinical and research focus is IBD, liver disease, and evidence-based digestive care.

I believe gastroenterology is a place where mind and body come together: physical symptoms can cause stress, and stress can cause physical symptoms. In a careful, attentive conversation, we can begin to understand how to treat both parts of the problem.

Background

Training & Experience

I completed my M.D. at the Hebrew University–Hadassah Medical School in Jerusalem (2006–2014), then trained as an intern at Shaare Zedek Medical Center, where I was recognized as Outstanding Intern in 2013. From 2014 to 2018 I completed my residency in internal medicine at Hadassah Mt. Scopus Hospital, serving as Chief Resident in my final year. After several years as a primary care physician with Meuhedet Health Services, I completed a fellowship in gastroenterology at Hadassah–Hebrew University Hospital (2020–2023), then served as a Senior Physician in the Department of Gastroenterology there and as a Gastroenterology Consultant with Meuhedet. I then spent time as an Advanced IBD Fellow at the Inflammatory Bowel Disease Center at the University of Chicago, working under Prof. David T. Rubin on advanced and combination therapy for medically complex inflammatory bowel disease. Today I am a senior physician in the gastroenterology department at Hadassah and at Meuhedet.
